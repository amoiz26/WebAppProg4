The Wild Duck

Act II - Hjalmar Ekdal's Studio

Hjalmar

Did he say anything?

Gina

No. What should he say?

Hjalmar

Didn't he say anything about—I thought I heard he'd been to Graberg's. I'll just go in to him a moment.

Gina

No, no, you'd better not.

Hjalmar

Why not? Did he say he wouldn't have me in there?

Gina

He won't have anyone in this evening.

Hedvig

H'm! h'm!

Gina does not see her.

Gina

He's been in here, and got warm water.

Hjalmar

Aha! He's sitting in there?

Gina

Yes, that's so.

Hjalmar

Good Heavens! My poor, white-haired old father?—Yes, just let him alone for once and enjoy himself.

Old Ekdal in an old coat, and with a lighted pipe, enters from his room.
